## Wording used in the brief (2026-09-11)

> South African online retail reached an estimated R130 billion in 2025 and is
> forecast to pass R159 billion this year, growing at over 20% a year. Total
> retail spending in South Africa was R1.53 trillion in 2025, so online is still
> only about a tenth of it. Our category is a large slice of that total:
> clothing, footwear and accessories retailers sold R236 billion in 2025, about
> 15% of all retail, up 5% on the year. Online is where the category's growth
> is: fashion is the largest online category, with online fashion sales
> estimated at around R20 billion and growing at 15–20% a year, three to four
> times the pace of the category as a whole.

Caveats: "clothing, footwear and accessories" stands in for Stats SA's
"textiles, clothing, footwear and leather goods". The R20 billion online
fashion figure blends two sources with different definitions. Category growth
of 5% is nominal (≈ 2% real).
