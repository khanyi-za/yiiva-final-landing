YIIVA Investor Brief
Khaziimla Technology (Pty) Ltd September 2026 · Private & confidential

YIIVA is the home of South African premium wear: the country's best independent brands, together in one app, where a shopper fills one cart, pays once, and the platform handles delivery and returns end to end.

This is not a deck-stage idea. The platform is built and running in production today, and the mobile app is in app-store launch preparation.

1 The problem & the market opportunity

South Africa has a deep bench of independent fashion and lifestyle brands (over 200 of them) with real products and real followings, and no infrastructure built for them. They sell through Instagram DMs and isolated web stores: discovery is accidental, checkout is friction-heavy, and every brand solves payments, delivery and returns alone. The large marketplaces are built for mass retail; a premium local label doesn't belong there, and the economics are hostile when it tries.

Buyers feel the same gap from the other side. The appetite for local premium wear is visibly growing, but shopping it means hopping between a dozen brand accounts and hoping checkout works. There is no destination. There is no central access point.

The backdrop: South African online retail has grown past an estimated R90 billion a year and continues to grow at double-digit rates, while still holding only a single-digit share of total retail. The structural runway is long, and fashion is consistently among the largest online categories. And the specific position is unclaimed: local premium brands, one place, one checkout.

2 Our solution

For buyers: the app is a digital mall: the central access point for premium local wear across every category. Shoppers walk in through a video-led discovery feed that mixes brands the way a great mall mixes storefronts, subscribe to the labels they love, fill one cart across many brands, and pay once. Delivery runs on a national courier network with stage-by-stage tracking; a 30-day returns process and platform-level buyer protection stand behind every order.

For brands: onboarding measured in minutes, not weeks. A brand connects its Shopify store and its full catalogue imports automatically, then stays in sync as prices and stock change. The brand gets a management dashboard on web and mobile, and the platform handles payments, courier collection and delivery end to end. Money never sits with us: every sale is split at the moment of payment and the brand's share settles directly to its own bank account on the next business day.

Where it stands today

Buyer app, merchant dashboards and admin operations are live in production. Payments are verified end-to-end with split settlement through Paystack; courier integration with The Courier Guy is live; the payment provider's compliance review is in its final stage; and we aim to launch the app this month on both the Google Play and Apple App Stores. As a proof of onboarding speed, we have imported the complete public catalogues of 28 real South African brands into our demonstration environment; importing an entire Shopify catalogue takes minutes.

3 The revenue model

  • Commission on every sale. Our launch rate is a deliberately low 2.5%, an early-adopter offer to win the first wave of brands, with a standard rate of around 5.5% on the roadmap as the network establishes.
  • Delivery. Charged to the buyer at cost today; a margin line as volume brings courier rates down.
  • Platform fee. Within the next 12 months, once our market presence is established, we aim to introduce a monthly platform fee of $43 (≈R690) per brand, comparable to what these brands already pay for standalone store tooling, but with the marketplace's audience included.
  • Built, not yet monetized. Promoted brand placement already exists inside the product (a weighting system in the discovery feed), and premium merchant tooling is a natural subscription tier once brands trade actively.

The cost base is software-margin: payments, courier and hosting scale with volume, and the platform runs lean by design.

4 Go-to-market

Brands first, personally. Before we pitch a brand, we import its real catalogue into a demonstration environment, so the first thing a founder sees is their own store, live inside YIIVA. Combined with minutes-long Shopify onboarding and the 2.5% early-adopter rate, the cost of saying yes is close to zero.

Buyers arrive with the brands. Each label that joins brings its social following with it: the app's subscribe mechanic is built to convert a brand's Instagram audience into a shopping audience. Video-first discovery matches how this audience already finds fashion.

Beachhead: South Africa's premium streetwear and design labels, concentrated in Cape Town and Johannesburg, then widening by category. The consumer app launches on the Google Play and Apple App Stores; the platform is live at yiiva.co.za.

The ask
